A top-down open-world action game: nine story missions, a five-star wanted system, street races, side jobs,
safehouses and businesses, day and night with rain, English and Vietnamese. Play in the browser:
https://quzkhanh.github.io/neon-harbor-web/

## What's new in 0.10.0

- A living street: pigeons, cats, dogs, seagulls and crabs wander the city (and can never be hurt, they just run or fly away). Quirky pedestrians too: a robot, an old man feeding pigeons.
- Pedestrians talk much more: many more lines, chatter between two people, and remarks about the rain, the night or your wanted level.
- Traffic accidents: two cars crash and block the road, traffic piles up honking, the drivers get out, argue, sometimes brawl, make up and drive away. Intersections no longer freeze cars forever.
- Night changes the streets instead of emptying them: fewer people, but drunks, night-shift workers and corner loiterers.
- The baseball bat is now clearly visible in your hands. Cars have head and tail lamps so you can tell front from back, and motorbikes show their rider.
- New "How to play" screen (main menu and pause menu): controls, healing, losing the cops, the police badge and more.

## Download

| System | File | How to run |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `NeonHarbor-*-windows.exe` | Double-click. The file is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn: click **More info → Run anyway**. |
| macOS 11+ (Intel and Apple Silicon) | `NeonHarbor-*-macos.zip` | Unzip and move **Neon Harbor.app** to Applications. The app is not notarized: the first time, right-click → **Open**; if macOS still refuses, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (or run `xattr -cr "/Applications/Neon Harbor.app"`). |
| Android 7+ (arm64 or x86_64) | `NeonHarbor-*-android.apk` | Open the file on the phone and allow installing apps from this source. Later versions install over this one and keep your save. |
| Linux (x86_64) | `NeonHarbor-*-linux.tar.gz` | `tar xzf NeonHarbor-*-linux.tar.gz && ./NeonHarbor.x86_64` |

Saves stay on your device. This is a beta: please report bugs in the Issues tab.
